# Branding

## CapstanAI

CapstanAI is the ecosystem and umbrella for governed multi-head AI workflow systems.

The name comes from capstan machinery:

- nautical capstan: controlled line-hauling under tension,
- engineering capstan/turret machine: modular multi-head staged work,
- project meaning: governed multi-head AI workflow system.

## CapstanAI - LabNote

CapstanAI - LabNote is the first simple ledger component: a manual, file-based lab notebook for AI session handoffs.

## WonderForge

WonderForge is the maker/studio mark. It can appear as a banner or seal, but it should not replace the product name.
